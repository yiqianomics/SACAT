# metaSPARSim simulation

This study evaluates SACAT with counts generated by metaSPARSim, using
Gamma-based abundance variation and read sampling without replacement.
It examines structural-absence (SA), relative-abundance (RA), and joint
signals under a generator distinct from SACAT's latent-state model.

## Design

Each replication has 152 controls, 91 cases, and 100 focal taxa plus a
background category. Target median depths are 5,855.5 and 3,678 reads,
following the Schubert CDI cohort.

The 38 settings comprise two global nulls with 500 replications each and
36 alternatives with 100 each. Alternatives affect 20 taxa, with or without
a standard-normal covariate generated independently of group and standardized
within each replication.

SA probability differences are 0.10, 0.15, 0.20, 0.25, 0.30, and 0.40,
evaluated at covariate value zero. RA log effects are 0.20, 0.40, 0.50,
0.60, 0.80, and 1.20. Joint effects pair these levels, with ten reinforcing
and ten opposing taxa. Exact settings and generator parameters are in
`data/design.csv` and `data/configuration.csv`.

## Figures and results

| PDF in `figures/` | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| [power.pdf](figures/power.pdf) | SA-only, RA-only, and joint-effect power |
| [fdr.pdf](figures/fdr.pdf) | False discovery rate (FDR) for the same comparisons |
| [type1_error.pdf](figures/type1_error.pdf) | Raw global-null Type I error for all 15 tests |

Power and FDR each have two pages. The first uses original p-values followed
by Benjamini–Hochberg (BH) adjustment at 0.05; the second uses matched-null
p-value calibration followed by BH. Columns distinguish effect scenarios,
and rows distinguish settings without and with a covariate.

SA panels compare SACAT SA with ZINQ and MaAsLin 3 prevalence tests. RA panels
use abundance tests. Joint panels use the package-native omnibus or joint
tests of SACAT, ZINQ, and MaAsLin 3. The six general-abundance methods appear
in both RA and joint panels.

Power is the proportion of affected taxa detected; FDR is the mean false
discovery proportion across replications. Type I error is the mean proportion
of null taxa with raw p < 0.05 across 500 replications, with intervals of
±1.96 Monte Carlo standard errors.

## Files and code

- `code/`: simulation, native joint-test reconstruction, summaries, and plotting.
- `data/`: raw p-values, component-specific truth, designs, generator parameters, and software versions.
- `tables/`: setting-level and replication-level results.
- `figures/`: the three PDFs above.

`tables/main_plot_data.csv` supplies the metaSPARSim panel of the
[main simulation figure](../MainFigures/README.md).

With `data.table`, `R.utils`, `ggplot2`, and `systemfonts` installed,
regenerate the tables and figures from this directory:

```sh
Rscript code/summarize_results.R
Rscript code/plot_results.R
```

`code/install_metasparsim.R` installs metaSPARSim 1.1.2 with R-seeded sampling
into the active R library. Comparison-package versions are recorded in
`data/software_versions.csv`.
